# brand/ file templates

Copy each block into its file. Delete any line you have no real fact for; leave a blank rather than a guess.

## brand/voice.md

```markdown
# Voice
Last updated: YYYY-MM-DD

Sounds like: <three words from the owner>
Person: <"I" / "we">   Sentence length: <short / mixed>   Emoji: <never / one at the end / often>
Words we use: <real words from their captions>
Words we never use: <from the owner, plus any they rejected later>
Spelling and format habits: <lowercase captions, numbers as digits, etc.>

## Sample captions (theirs, unedited)
1. "<caption 1>"
2. "<caption 2>"
3. "<caption 3>"

## Notes
```
